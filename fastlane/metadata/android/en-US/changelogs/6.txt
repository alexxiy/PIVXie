Privacy: copied recovery phrases and keys are marked as sensitive, so Android hides them in the copy preview and keyboards leave them out of their clipboard history. Wallets are no longer copied to a new phone or a cloud backup, and the QR scanner no longer sends usage statistics to Google. Cold staking and governance links to the block explorer now warn you first. Deleting a wallet now removes all of its history. The PIN screen and other pages now fit tablets in landscape.
