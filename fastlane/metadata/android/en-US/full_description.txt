PIVXie is a non-custodial mobile wallet for PIVX. Your recovery phrase and keys never leave your phone, and nobody else can move your coins.

Highlights:
- SHIELD private transactions: shield, unshield and send privately, with an optional encrypted memo.
- Cold staking: delegate PIV to a staker and earn staking rewards while your coins stay in your wallet. Pick a staker from masternode.space or use your own node, and undelegate at any time.
- Masternodes: set up a masternode from your wallet, start it, and follow its status and rewards.
- Governance: browse budget proposals, vote with your masternodes, submit your own proposal, and see when budget payments arrive.
- Clear history: staking rewards, masternode rewards and proposal payments are labelled, and you can filter by each type.
- Coin control, an address book and pivx: payment links.
- Encrypted backups of all your wallets and settings, plus automatic backups.
- PIN and fingerprint lock.
- Optional Tor for every connection.
- Connect to your own pivx-bridge server if you prefer.
- Open source (GPLv3), with no ads and no tracking.
