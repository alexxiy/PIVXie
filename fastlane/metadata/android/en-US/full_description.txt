PIVXie is a PIVX wallet that keeps you in control. Your recovery phrase and keys stay on your device. There's no account to create, and nobody else can move your coins.

Privacy
• SHIELD transactions: shield, unshield and send PIV privately, with an optional encrypted memo.
• Optional Tor for every connection.
• No ads and no tracking.

Staking, masternodes and governance
• Cold staking: delegate your PIV to a staker and receive staking rewards while the coins stay in your wallet. Pick a staker from masternode.space or use your own node, and undelegate whenever you like.
• Masternodes: set up a masternode from the wallet, start it, and follow its status and rewards.
• Governance: browse budget proposals, vote with your masternodes, submit your own proposal and see when budget payments arrive.

PIVX Name
• Register an easy name like yourname.pivx (or .private, .secure, .safe) and share it instead of a long address.
• Point your name to any SHIELD address, yours or someone else's, and change it whenever you like. People keep paying the same name.
• Give a name to someone else, or sell your names on the marketplace and buy the ones you like.
• Your names belong to your recovery phrase, so the same phrase opens them on pivx.name too.
• Pay to a PIVX name instead of an address. PIVXie checks where the name points before you send.

Everyday use
• Scan QR codes and open pivx: payment links.
• Address book and coin control.
• Sign and verify messages.
• A clear history: staking rewards, masternode rewards, proposal payments and SHIELD transactions are labelled, and you can filter by each kind.
• Several accounts from one recovery phrase, and watch-only wallets from an extended public key.

Backups and security
• Restore with your 12- or 24-word recovery phrase, with an optional passphrase. Recovery phrases from MyPIVXWallet work too.
• Encrypted backups of your wallets and settings, plus automatic backups.
• PIN and biometric lock, and a duress PIN that opens the app with only the wallets you choose.

Your own server, if you like
PIVXie connects to the PIVXie bridge by default. You can switch to your own pivx-bridge server at any time.

Keep your recovery phrase written down somewhere safe. Whoever has it controls your coins, and nobody, the PIVXie team included, can restore it for you.
